removed genres
Rows whose poetId is in no poets.csv, so getGenres() returned nothing for them and
they were dead. Aristotle and Castorion were among the poet stubs removed at the top
of this file; Eunicus and Kinesias have never been in poets.csv at all, in any
revision.

Kinesias is archived as it stood, poetId and all, rather than repointed at Cinesias
(poetId 4). Kinesias and Cinesias are both Κινησίας, and Pherecrates fr. 155 is
about the Athenian dithyrambist the Suda entry on Cinesias also describes, so the
two may well be one man. But nothing in this project ever said so: poetId 14 has
never had a row in poets.csv. Where the project does hold that two names may be one
poet it says so in the name and keeps both, as with Kydides (47) and "Cydias
(Kydides?)" (106), which share Stefanis 1520 between them. Merging the two here
would assert an identification the project has not made, so the row waits instead.
